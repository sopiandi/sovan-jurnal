# November 16, 2026 - Backing Up the Shop's Records

A phone can be lost, dropped, or stolen in a single afternoon. The
records on it — prices, stock counts, customer balances — are worth
far more than the phone itself. Losing them means starting over from
memory, and memory is the least reliable record of all.

A backup is simply a second copy kept somewhere else. For a small shop,
the habit matters more than the tool. A few rules keep it simple:

- Copy the important records regularly, not only when something breaks.
- Keep one copy away from the shop, so one accident cannot take both.
- Test the copy sometimes. A backup that cannot be restored is decoration.

The cost is a few minutes a week. The cost of having no backup is
rebuilding months of careful work from scratch, while customers wait
and questions pile up. A shop that can lose its records in one bad day
is a shop standing on one leg. Two copies make it stand on two.
