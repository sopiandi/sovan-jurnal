# November 2, 2026 - From a Paper Notebook to an App

For years, shop records lived in a paper notebook: stock lists, prices,
and who owed what. It worked, but adding long columns by hand was slow,
erasing a paid debt left smudges, and one lost notebook meant lost history.

Moving those records into a simple app changed three things:

- Totals add themselves, so arithmetic mistakes mostly disappear.
- Old entries stay searchable instead of fading under new pages.
- Two phones can see the same records at the same time.

The trade-offs are real too: a phone battery dies, a notebook does not,
and a new habit takes weeks to feel normal. The lesson is not that digital
is always better. It is that each tool fits a different weakness, and the
switch is worth it when the old weakness is the one costing you money.
