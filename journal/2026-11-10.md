# November 10, 2026 - What Happens When Someone Buys Phone Credit

When a customer buys phone credit at a small shop, a chain of computers
wakes up. The shop sends an order with the phone number and the amount
to a server. The server checks the shop balance, passes the order to the
mobile provider, and the provider adds the credit. The whole trip often
takes seconds.

Prices differ slightly between shops for ordinary reasons:

- Each shop buys its balance at a slightly different cost.
- Small orders carry almost the same work as big ones.
- Shops price in the risk of failed orders and refunds.

Seen from the counter it looks instant and simple. Behind it sits a
deposit the shop paid in advance, which is why selling credit is also a
small act of cash management, not just pressing a button.
