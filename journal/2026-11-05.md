# November 5, 2026 - A Price Is Not a Sale

A screen can show a price for almost anything. That number only means
someone, somewhere, traded at that price once, or that a formula guessed
it. It does not promise that you can sell at that price today.

Real selling needs a market with actual buyers and money waiting in it.
Traders call that depth liquidity. A brand-new token can show a price
that looks exciting while the pool of money behind it is tiny, and the
moment holders try to sell together, the price collapses because there
is simply nobody on the other side.

The habit worth keeping: whenever a number on a screen looks like money,
ask one question before celebrating. Can this actually be turned into
cash, right now, at close to that number? If the answer is unknown, the
honest value is unknown too, no matter what the screen says.
