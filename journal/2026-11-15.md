# November 15, 2026 - Learning by Trying

Most useful skills in a small business were not learned from a course.
They were learned by trying something small, watching it fail or work,
and adjusting. The trick is to keep the experiments small enough that
failing is cheap.

A method that helps:

- Change one thing at a time, so the result has a clear cause.
- Write down what happened, even in one line.
- Give a change a fair trial period before judging it.

That is also the honest reason for this journal. Writing one small note
a day forces the lessons out of the head and onto a page, where they
can be reread, questioned, and shared. Experience fades. Notes stay.
