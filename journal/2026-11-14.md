# November 14, 2026 - Shop Credit, Written Fairly

Letting regular customers take goods now and pay later is an old habit
of neighbourhood shops. It keeps loyal customers coming back, and it
works on trust. Trust works better when it is written down.

A fair record for shop credit has only a few pieces:

- The customer's name, written the same way every time.
- The date and what was taken.
- Each payment, with its date, and the balance after it.

Writing it down is not a sign of suspicion. It protects both sides. The
customer can check the balance is right, and the shop can show its work
when memories disagree. Most credit arguments are not about dishonesty.
They are about two honest people remembering different numbers, and a
notebook settles that in seconds.
