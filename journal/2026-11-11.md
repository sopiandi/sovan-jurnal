# November 11, 2026 - The Simple Cash Book

Every good money system in a small business can be traced back to three
columns: money in, money out, and what is left. A cash book does not
need software. It needs honesty and a daily habit.

Rules that keep a cash book useful:

- Write entries the same day, while memory is fresh.
- Separate the shop money from household money, even on paper.
- When the counted cash disagrees with the book, write the difference
  down instead of quietly fixing it. A written gap can be explained
  later. A hidden one just grows.

Apps make this faster, and faster helps the habit survive busy days.
But the habit itself is the technology. The columns came first, long
before any phone did.
