# November 8, 2026 - Why the Speedometer Lies

A speed test can promise a fast connection while real work still crawls.
Speed is only one part of the story. Stability matters just as much: a
connection that drops to zero ten times an hour feels worse than a
slower connection that never drops.

A few plain truths about shop internet:

- Evening hours are slower because the whole neighbourhood is online.
- A modem placed low and behind goods gets a weaker signal.
- One phone uploading a big video can starve every other device.

Before paying for a bigger package, it is worth watching when the slow
moments actually happen. Half the time, the fix is moving the modem,
restarting it on a schedule, or shifting heavy uploads to quiet hours,
not buying more speed.
