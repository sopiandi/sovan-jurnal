# November 12, 2026 - What the Cloud Means for a Small Shop

The cloud sounds abstract, but for a small shop it means something very
concrete: your records live on computers run by a big company, so every
phone you sign in on sees the same records, updated at the same moment.

What this buys a two-person business:

- The owner can check the day from home while a partner runs the shop.
- A broken phone no longer means broken records.
- There is nothing to carry back and forth to keep copies in sync.

What it asks in return:

- An internet connection, at least now and then.
- A password worth protecting, because the sign-in is now the key to
  the whole shop.

Paper never needed a password, but paper also never appeared on two
counters at once.
