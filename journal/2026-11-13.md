# November 13, 2026 - Choosing a Work Phone

A work phone for a small business has a different job from a phone for
entertainment. The screen is read in bright daylight, the battery must
survive a full trading day, and the camera doubles as a barcode scanner
and a receipt camera.

Priorities that actually matter at a counter:

- Battery size over thin design.
- A bright screen over a high-resolution one.
- Enough storage that updates never fight for space.
- A case and a screen guard, because counters are hard surfaces.

Chasing the newest model rarely pays for itself in a shop. A mid-range
phone that is fast enough, readable in sunlight, and still alive at
closing time is the professional tool. The rest is decoration.
